We published **Semente** expecting zero feedback — it was an 18-card exercise, not a "launch."

Within two weeks we got over thirty photos of hand-made boards, two unofficial remixes, and a request to translate it into German. That changed how we think about print & play releases: they're no longer "free samples" — they're the fastest way to find out if a game has legs before investing in a physical box.
