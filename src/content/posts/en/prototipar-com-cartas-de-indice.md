After **Engrenagens do Vale** cost us weeks of iterating on pretty components too early, we tried the opposite: our last three prototypes were built with nothing but index cards and a pen.

It worked well for testing mechanics. It worked poorly for testing "game feel" — some ideas only reveal their real problem once components feel final. The practical takeaway: index cards for the first five sessions, then invest in real components.
