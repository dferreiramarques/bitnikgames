In **Rio de Flores**, each player runs a small barge trading flowers along a river to riverside villages. The modular board changes every game, so the most profitable routes are never quite the same twice.

It's the most "gateway" game in our catalog — rules explained in five minutes, real decisions every turn.

## What's in the box

- 1 modular board (6 tiles)
- 60 flower cards
- 4 wooden barges
- Rulebook (PT/EN)
