Sleepy Cat is a fast, fun, and unpredictable game of skill and reflexes for 2 or more players. The goal is simple: toss the Sleepy Cat to other players and try to score 20 points before anyone else.

On each turn, the player chooses who will try to catch the cat. If the cat lands in the player's hands, the current score increases: 1 point if it lands tail-side up, or 2 points if it lands belly-side up. But be careful — if the cat touches the player's hands and then falls to the floor, the player who made the throw claims all the points accumulated so far!

If the cat doesn't touch anyone's hands and falls directly to the floor, the score is reset to zero.

Everything depends on your throws, the other players' reflexes, and, of course, a little bit of luck. No intentional grabbing — every catch must be a fair attempt!

Can you keep the score climbing without letting the Sleepy Cat slip away? The first player to reach 20 points wins!
